Help & How-To

Everything below happens right on your home screen — there's nothing extra to install or set up.

login Logging In
  1. Go to your location's page (bookmark it, or ask your manager for the link).
  2. Enter your 6-digit code in the "Employee Clock In" box.
  3. Press Login.

Don't know your code, or think it's wrong? Ask your manager to reset it, or use Reset My Code from your account menu (top right, once you're logged in) if you're already signed in on another device.

alarm_on Clocking In
  1. After logging in, find today's shift under This Week's Schedule.
  2. Press the Clock In button in the "Clock In / Clock Out" column.

free_breakfast Breaks & Lunch
  1. Once you're clocked in, the Breaks column shows + Break and + Lunch buttons.
  2. Press the one that matches what you're starting.
  3. When you're back, press End next to that break/lunch to close it out.

logout Clocking Out
  1. End any open break or lunch first (see above).
  2. Press Clock Out in the "Clock In / Clock Out" column.

Once you clock out, your hours worked for that shift appear right below the punch times.

tips_and_updates Good to Know
  • Punching in/out is self-service — only you can punch your own shift.
  • If you miss a punch or something looks wrong, tell your manager — they can correct it for you.
  • Your schedule, breaks, and hours all update live, so it's safe to refresh the page any time.
  • Use the account menu (top right) to switch to dark mode, change the theme color, view your documents, or check your availability and schedule.
