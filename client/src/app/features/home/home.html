Questions

Frequently asked

Do my employees need to download an app?

No. TAT runs in any web browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. Many teams set up one shared tablet as a kiosk where everyone punches in with a PIN.

Can I stop people clocking in from home?

Yes. You can limit punches to approved devices or your location's network, and set how many minutes before a shift someone is allowed to clock in.

Does it handle overtime and double time?

Yes. Each location sets its own daily and weekly overtime, double time, and seventh-consecutive-day rules, and the payroll report applies them for you.

How does it work with my payroll provider?

The payroll report totals regular, overtime, double time, and sick hours per employee for any pay period, ready to enter into your payroll system.

We have more than one location. Is that extra work?

No. Every location has its own roster, settings, and reports, all run from one account.

How long does it take to get started?

Usually a few days. Import your employees from a spreadsheet, set up your shifts, and start scheduling — we walk you through it.
